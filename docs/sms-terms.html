Ping — SMS Notifications & Consent

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Ping is an event-planning app. This page explains when and why Ping sends text messages, and how to stop receiving them.

How you end up getting a text from Ping

You are not signed up for texts, and Ping does not send marketing or bulk messages. A text only goes out when someone who already has your phone number saved as a contact uses Ping to invite you to a specific event, and you don't yet have a linked Ping account for us to notify you in-app instead. That one action — being personally invited to a specific event by someone who has your number — is what triggers the message.

What the messages look like

Invite message (sent once, when you're invited to an event):

You're invited to "Family Party!"! Saturday, August 8 at 6:30 PM — 123 Main St. Reply YES or NO to RSVP. https://piersonw.github.io/Ping/invite.html?i=...

Confirmation message (sent once, after you reply):

Thanks! You're marked as going for "Family Party!". See full details and get the Ping app: https://piersonw.github.io/Ping/invite.html?i=...

Message frequency

Irregular — only when someone personally invites you to an event, plus one reply confirming your RSVP. There is no recurring or scheduled messaging.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts from Ping. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
